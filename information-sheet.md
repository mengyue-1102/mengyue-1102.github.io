# Information Sheet for Participants
Project: Usability evaluation of XJTLU Major Selection AI Assistant
Course: ADS401

### What is this study about?
We test an AI agent that helps students select XJTLU majors. We want to know user experience and usability problems.

### What you need to do
You will complete simple tasks with the AI tool, finish a short survey and a short interview. Total time: 10-15 minutes.

### Risks & benefits
There is no physical risk. You can learn about XJTLU major information from this AI.

### Your rights
1. Your participation is VOLUNTARY. You can stop at any time, no penalty.
2. All your data will be anonymous. We will NOT collect your name, student ID or private information.
3. All data is only used for ADS401 course assignment. We will NOT use it for commercial use.

### Contact
If you have questions, you can contact our group.
