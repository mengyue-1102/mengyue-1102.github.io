# Interview Questions for XJTLU Major Selection AI Assistant
Semi-structured interview for usability evaluation

Background warm-up
1. What is your plan for choosing university majors?
2. Before this test, have you known about XJTLU undergraduate majors?

Task-based questions
3. When you used this AI assistant to search for majors, was it easy for you?
4. Did the AI give clear and useful answers about XJTLU majors?
5. What part confused you when chatting with this AI?

Overall experience
6. Do you think this AI tool can help students choose majors? Why or why not?
7. What functions do you hope to add to this AI assistant?
8. Any other comments or suggestions?
